NYC art galleries over the holidays

If you're in New York between Thanksgiving and New Year's, the city's art galleries are one of the best free things to do: warm, quiet, never ticketed, and often a few blocks from the crowds. The catch is the calendar. Galleries close on the holidays, and plenty take a longer break between Christmas and New Year's.

This page tracks it. We follow 956 walk-in galleries across the five boroughs, and we check the holiday hours each one posts on Google. Google only shows holiday hours about a week ahead, so each holiday below fills in as it gets close.

What to expect

Thanksgiving weekend. Treat Thanksgiving Day as closed. Many galleries also take Black Friday off, and some stay closed through the weekend. Museums are the better bet on Thursday and Friday.

December. The first three weeks are a good time to go. Shows are up, openings slow down and the rooms are quiet. Many exhibitions close around the middle of the month, and some galleries spend the last weeks of December between shows.

Christmas to New Year's. This is when the most galleries are closed. Some shut for the whole week. The ones that stay open often run shorter hours. Check before you make a special trip.

January. The new season starts in the second week of January, with a run of openings, usually on Thursday evenings.
