How we know. Every day we fetch the website of each gallery on the map and compare it with the copy we saw the day before. When a page changes, that gallery gets flagged here and shows up green on the map for eight days. It's an imperfect signal — a gallery that never updates its site won't appear here even if it has a superb show up — but it's the closest thing to a live pulse of the city's gallery calendar that we know of.
