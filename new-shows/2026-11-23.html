296 of the 807 galleries we track changed something on their website in the past seven days — about 37% of the map. That's well above the 103.4 we normally see in a week, and usually means a wave of openings rather than routine housekeeping.

Manhattan led the week with 255 galleries showing site changes, followed by Brooklyn (33), Queens (6), Bronx (1), Staten Island (1). A change can mean a new exhibition going up, a run of dates being extended, or simply a fresh set of images.

For 109 of the 296 we could read what was new: headings that appeared on the gallery's homepage this week, which is usually a show title or an artist's name. Those are listed under the gallery. For the rest we can only tell that the page changed, so treat them as a shortlist worth checking rather than a guarantee of a new show.

This is the busiest week since we started keeping this archive 9 weeks ago, beating the previous high of 208.
